# SparkStart & SparkBuddy Demonstration Script

**Estimated Duration:** 4–5 minutes  
**Target Audience:** Hackathon Judges, University Evaluators, Peer Reviewers

---

## Act 1: The Problem & The Non-Judgmental Greeting (0:00 – 1:00)

1. **Open the Web Dashboard** at `http://localhost:5173`.
2. **Highlight the Philosophy**:
   > *"Most productivity tools bombard users with 25 overdue tasks, aggressive red notifications, and broken streak warnings. For university students experiencing cognitive fatigue or executive dysfunction, this causes task freeze."*
3. **Show Energy Check-In**:
   - Point out the 4 energy levels: *Low Energy (2–3 min starter)*, *Medium Energy (5–10 min)*, *Higher Capacity*, and *Not Sure / Skip*.
   - Click **Low Energy**. Note that the user can skip or change this at any time with zero shame.

---

## Act 2: The Task Sequencer & RAG Literature Grounding (1:00 – 2:30)

1. **Enter an Intimidating Task**:
   - Type *"Complete DBMS Assignment 3 on B+ Trees and Joins"* into the task field.
   - In the optional block field, write *"Don't know where to begin"*.
   - Click **Find My Next Step**.
2. **Observe the Sequenced Micro-Action**:
   - The card appears with a single target: *"Open the assignment PDF and read just question number 1."*
   - Point to the **Approx. 2 mins** badge.
   - Expand the **Grounded in Evidence** accordion:
     - Show the citation: *Gollwitzer, P. M. (1999) on Implementation Intentions and Threshold Reduction*.
     - Explain: *"SparkStart doesn't hallucinate arbitrary steps; its RAG engine matches cognitive strategies to the student's stated obstacle."*

---

## Act 3: The "Too Big" Shrink & Guilt-Free Rest (2:30 – 3:30)

1. **Demonstrate Resistance Handling**:
   - Click the amber **Too Big** button.
   - The sequencer immediately responds with an ultra-mini step:
     - *"Don't read anything yet. Just open the PDF, glance at the title, and take 3 deep breaths (1 min)."*
   - Explain how this dissolves perfectionist anxiety.
2. **Demonstrate Guilt-Free Rest**:
   - Click **Guilt-Free Rest**.
   - The calming recovery modal opens with the resting sprout mascot and animated breathing ring (*Inhale 4s... Hold 4s... Exhale 4s*).
   - Point out that there is no punitive alarm or forced return to work.

---

## Act 4: SparkBuddy Floating Desktop Companion (3:30 – 4:30)

1. **Switch to VS Code or Browser**:
   - Show SparkBuddy floating in the corner of the desktop over code and browser windows.
   - Point out the original cartoon sprout mascot and its friendly speech bubble.
   - Click **Done**: The sprout celebrates with happy sparkle eyes!
   - Demonstrate dragging the mascot anywhere on the screen and opening the Mini-Settings to verify **Always-on-Top**.

---

## Act 5: Local Privacy & Data Sovereignty (4:30 – 5:00)

1. **Navigate to Settings & Privacy**:
   - Show the **Remote AI Processing Consent** toggle:
     - Point out that by default, SparkStart runs 100% locally with zero cloud data transmission.
   - Click **Export My Data (JSON)**: Demonstrates immediate file download of all local tasks.
   - Click **Erase All Local Data**: Demonstrates instant, verifiable SQLite database purge.
2. **Concluding Takeaway**:
   > *"SparkStart and SparkBuddy: Small steps. Real progress. Productivity that respects human autonomy."*
